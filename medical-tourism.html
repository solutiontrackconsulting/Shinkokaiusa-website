Medical Tourism Japan

医療観光プログラム

A 10-day, fully coordinated health screening and medical care program in Japan — with interpretation, logistics, and wellness programming included.

「通訳・手配・ウェルネスをすべて含む、10日間の完全サポート付き健診・医療体験プログラム。」

Program coordination fee: $5,500–$15,000  ·  10-day package  ·  Medical facility fees billed separately

「プログラム調整費：$5,500〜$15,000（10日間）· 医療施設費は別途」

HOW IT WORKS
ご支援の流れ

Medical Tourism Japan is a 10-day, fully coordinated health and wellness program. We design your personalized medical itinerary, manage all logistics, and provide interpretation at every appointment — so you can focus entirely on your health.

「医療観光プログラムは、10日間の完全サポート付き健診・ウェルネスプログラムです。個別スケジュールの設計から全手配・通訳まで、すべてをコーディネートいたします。」

This is a consultation and logistics coordination service. All clinical care is provided by licensed physicians and facilities in Japan. We do not provide medical services directly.

「本プログラムは相談・調整サービスです。すべての臨床医療は、日本の認定施設・医師が提供します。」

Start with a Free Consultation
THE PROCESS
ステップ

Five steps from first call to your Japan health experience

「最初のご相談から日本での健康体験まで、五つのステップ」

  1. 01

    Free Initial Consultation

    無料初回相談

    We begin with a complimentary consultation to understand your health goals, medical history, and preferred travel window. We explain the Medical Tourism Japan program in full and help you determine whether it is the right fit. There is no obligation.

    「まず無料の初回相談で、健康目標・病歴・ご希望の渡航時期をお聞きします。プログラムの詳細をご説明し、お客様のご状況に合っているかを一緒に確認します。」

  2. 02

    Program Design & Appointment Coordination

    プログラム設計・予約調整

    Based on your health profile, we design a personalized 10-day medical itinerary and coordinate appointments at licensed facilities in Japan. Depending on your goals, this may include a ningen dock (comprehensive health screening), PET scan, MRI, dental assessment, or specialist second-opinion consultations.

    「健康プロファイルをもとに、10日間の個別スケジュールを設計し、日本の認定施設への予約調整を行います。人間ドック、PETスキャン、MRI、歯科診断、専門医によるセカンドオピニオンなどを組み合わせることができます。」

  3. 03

    Travel & Logistics Coordination

    渡航・滞在の手配

    We coordinate all logistics for your Japan stay — airport pickup and drop-off, accommodation near your medical facilities, ground transport between appointments, and professional interpretation services throughout. You travel; we handle the details.

    「日本での滞在に必要なすべての手配をいたします。空港の送迎、医療施設近くの宿泊手配、予約間の移動、そして滞在を通じたプロの通訳サービスをご提供します。」

  4. 04

    Your 10-Day Japan Health Experience

    日本での10日間の体験

    Our team accompanies you through each appointment — interpreting with physicians, assisting with paperwork, and helping you feel at ease throughout. Depending on your program, your itinerary may also include traditional hot spring (onsen) therapy and a guided cultural experience as part of your wellness stay.

    「各診療への通訳同行・書類サポートを行います。プログラムによっては、温泉療法や文化体験も含まれ、健康と療養を兼ねた充実した滞在をお楽しみいただけます。」

  5. 05

    Results Support & Follow-Up

    結果説明・フォローアップ

    After your return to the United States, we assist with interpreting and translating your medical reports so you can share them clearly with your US physicians. We can also help coordinate any follow-up care or next steps you may need.

    「帰国後も、医療レポートや検査結果の解釈・翻訳をサポートし、米国の主治医へのスムーズな引き継ぎをお手伝いします。次のステップや継続ケアの手配もサポートいたします。」

Your Japan health experience starts with a conversation.
